Most people navigate life by guessing.

We have learned so much about how things *should* be done — how to learn, how to decide, how to succeed, how to love — that many of us no longer know who we actually are. We build our lives on rules and habits that were never made for us, and wonder why it keeps feeling like hard work.

**reloom** is a place to find your way back. Give it the moment you were born — date, time, place — and it hands you the blueprint that was there all along: a clear, warm map of who you are and how you are built to live, work, decide and connect. No prior knowledge. No expert required. Readable the same day.

It lives at **[reloom.life](https://reloom.life)**. Free to begin, no credit card, your data stays yours.

## What the name means

*re·loom* — to take the loose threads of who you are and weave them, patiently, into something whole.

That is the whole idea in one word. Not a makeover, not a new you. A **re**-weaving of what was already there: the parts of yourself you set aside because they did not fit the pattern you were handed. And because threads are never woven alone, reloom also means **togetherness** — a reconnection first to yourself, and through that to everyone around you.

## Why I started it

reloom began as something I built for myself.

I have spent most of my working life helping companies make clear decisions with technology. Somewhere along the way I noticed how rarely that clarity reached the person making the decision: *is this true for me?* Human Design gave me language for that — for how I take in the world, how I am meant to decide, why some rooms give me energy and others quietly drain it. But every tool I tried handed me a chart full of symbols and left me to do the translating.

So I built the reading I wished existed: one that explains your design the way a good friend would, in plain language, without the jargon in the way. Then I added the people I love — and the readings about *us* turned out to be the ones I kept coming back to. Then the people I work with. Then the practitioners who asked if they could use it with their clients.

That is how a personal project became reloom. The ambition grew; the reason did not change. **I wanted something that helps people remember who they are.**

## What Human Design is, in human terms

Human Design is a map of how you are built. It takes your exact birth moment and draws a **Bodygraph** — a picture of where you are consistent and where you are open, how energy moves through you, and how you are designed to make decisions that are right for you.

It gives you a **Type** (how you are meant to move through the world), a **Strategy** (how to meet life so it meets you back) and an **Authority** (where in you the truth of a decision lives — for some it is the gut, for others the heart, for others time).

What makes it different from the personality tests most of us have taken at work: it does not describe how you *tend to behave*. It describes **how you are actually built** — the foundation underneath everything you were taught.

## Why so many people are looking for this now

For generations we were taught that there is one right way to live. Most of us built our lives on those borrowed rules — and lost sight of ourselves in the process.

A quiet shift is underway. People are no longer asking *"How should I live?"* They are asking **"How am I built to live?"** — when choosing a study, when a career stops fitting, in a new relationship, raising a child, after a burnout, at every fresh start.

And the hard part has moved. Getting a Human Design chart used to be the obstacle; today a chart is free and instant. The real question is no longer *"Can I see my chart?"* but **"Can I understand it — and use it on a Monday morning?"** That gap, between a chart full of symbols and a life actually lived from it, is exactly where reloom lives.

## It starts with you — and it doesn't stop there

reloom is built in layers, and each one invites the next.

**First, yourself.** You discover your own blueprint — the foundation that was factory-installed all along, hidden underneath everything you were taught. That first reading opens a door.

**Then the other.** You add your partner, your child, your mother, your best friend. You read how *they* are built, and something softens: the thing that always annoyed you turns out to be design, not disrespect.

**Then the space between you.** reloom reads the relationship itself — where you click, where the friction comes from, what each of you brings the other, and how to say things so they land. As partners, as parent and child, as colleagues.

**Then the whole.** Add a team or a family and reloom maps the dynamics of the group: who complements whom, where the energy flows, where it gets stuck. For companies and HR teams this is the piece that changes how people are placed and paired.

Daily life keeps the door open. A difficult conversation, a career decision, a pattern that keeps repeating — each one becomes a reason to look again, understand what just happened, and **reloom** the interaction: to relearn how you connect, this time from who you actually are.

## Insight is not the point. Transformation is.

A reading that only makes you feel seen changes nothing. reloom is built for the moments where life actually happens — a career switch, a new relationship, a child, a burnout, a fresh start. In those moments it does not hand you theory. It helps you:

- **Remember who you are** — your own foundation, in plain human language
- **Make decisions that are true for you** — not just sensible for everyone
- **Rebuild the way you live, work and connect** — from your design, not from borrowed rules

reloom does not do the growing for anyone. It moves the plant to the water. From there, people, practitioners and — in time — a whole community do the rest.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is reloom?
A Human Design platform at [reloom.life](https://reloom.life). You enter your birth moment — date, time and place — and receive a living Bodygraph chart and warm, plain-language readings about who you are and how you are built to live, work, decide and connect. No prior knowledge, no practitioner required.

### What does the name mean?
*re·loom*: to take the loose threads of who you are and weave them, patiently, into something whole. It also means togetherness — a reconnection first to yourself, and through that to everyone around you.

### What is Human Design?
A map of how you are built, drawn from your exact birth moment. It shows how you take in energy, how you are designed to make decisions, and how you connect with others — through a Type, a Strategy and an Authority. Unlike personality tests, it describes how you are *built*, not how you tend to behave.

### Is reloom free?
Yes, to begin. The Free plan includes your own chart and three readings a month, no credit card. Personal (€9), Practitioner (€29) and Studio (€79) per month add more people, more readings and more reading types — [see the plans](https://reloom.life/pricing).

### Can it read relationships and teams, not just me?
Yes. reloom reads the space between two people — eighteen kinds of relationship, from romantic and parent–child to manager and report — and maps the dynamics of a whole team or family: who complements whom, where the friction comes from, and how to communicate with each person.

### Who built it?
I did — Ray Bogman — in 2026. It started as a personal project and grew into a platform for individuals, practitioners and organisations. [More about me](/about/).

### Does it work with WordPress?
Yes. The free **[Reloom for Human Design](/products/reloom-human-design/)** plugin brings charts and readings into wp-admin for practitioners, connected to the same reloom account in one click.
