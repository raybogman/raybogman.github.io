**Reloom for Human Design** is a free WordPress plugin for people who help others understand themselves. It puts your clients' **Human Design charts** and **plain-language readings** in the WordPress dashboard you already use — so a session starts with a chart you can open, a reading you can read aloud, and a PDF you can hand over.

## First: what is Human Design?

Human Design is a map of how you are built. Give it the moment you were born — date, time and place — and it draws a **Bodygraph**: a picture of how you take in energy, how you are meant to make decisions, where you are consistent and where you are open to the people around you. It gives you a **Type**, a **Strategy** and an **Authority** — in plain words: how you are designed to move through the world, and how to know when something is right for you.

What makes it different from most personality tools is this: it does not describe how you tend to behave. It describes **how you are actually built** — the foundation that was there all along, underneath everything you were taught about how you *should* live.

## Why so many people are looking for it right now

For generations we were taught that there is one right way to live: one way to learn, one way to decide, one way to succeed. Most of us built our lives on those borrowed rules — and somewhere along the way lost sight of who we actually are.

A quiet shift is underway. People are no longer asking *"How should I live?"* They are asking **"How am I built to live?"** — in career choices, relationships, parenting, burnout, and every fresh start in between. Awareness of Human Design is growing faster than access to good interpretation. Getting a chart is free and instant today; **understanding it** is the hard part. Until now that meant an expensive expert, years of study, or a chart full of symbols with no explanation.

That gap is where practitioners live — and it is exactly the gap this plugin was built to close.

## Where it makes a difference

The moments where a design matters most are the moments where life actually happens:

- **Choosing a study** — a seventeen-year-old picking a direction based on grades and expectations, when their design says something very different about how they learn and what gives them energy
- **Choosing a profession** — not *which* job pays best, but *how* you are built to work: alone or alongside others, initiating or responding, in bursts or at a steady pace
- **Good times** — knowing your design helps you say yes to the right things and build on what is already working, instead of chasing what works for everyone else
- **Bad times** — a burnout, a break-up, a job that drains you. Your design tells you what you have been doing against your own grain, and what to stop doing first
- **Relationships and parenting** — understanding why your partner decides differently, why one child needs to be asked and the other needs to be invited, and how to meet them there

And it does not stop at the individual. This is where reloom is genuinely different from a chart tool: it reads **the space between people**. Add the people you work with — or your family — and reloom analyses the **dynamics of the whole group**: who complements whom, where the friction will come from, and **how to communicate with each person** in the way they are built to receive it. One colleague needs to be asked, another needs time to respond, a third needs to see it before they believe it. The same holds at the kitchen table.

**Companies and HR teams** are starting to use this the way they once used personality assessments — to place people where they truly fit, to put the right working pairs together, and to understand why a team that looks perfect on paper keeps colliding. Every person who understands their own blueprint carries it into their family, their team and their work.

## How the idea was born

reloom started as something I built for myself: a way to turn a birth moment into a reading I could actually use on a Monday morning, written the way a good friend would explain it instead of a textbook. Then I added the people I love. Then the people I work with.

What I kept hearing from the Human Design practitioners who tried it was the same thing: *"This is the reading I wish I could hand my clients — but my practice lives on WordPress."* The booking page, the blog, the intake form — all WordPress. The charts, though, lived somewhere else: another app, a folder of PDFs, a browser tab that never closes.

So instead of asking practitioners to come to reloom, I brought reloom to them. (Why I started reloom at all is in [this post](/blog/why-i-built-reloom/).) **Reloom for Human Design** puts the client roster, the charts and the readings inside wp-admin, in sync with their reloom account. No second login. No copy-paste. The reading is the hero; the tools stay in the background.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the plugin free?
Yes. The plugin is free on WordPress.org, and the reloom **Free** plan is enough to connect a site, cast your own chart and try it. Paid plans — Personal, Practitioner, Studio — add more people, more readings and more reading types. [See the plans](https://reloom.life/pricing).

### Do I need an API key or a separate AI subscription?
No. The plugin never talks to a chart engine or an AI provider directly. It connects to your reloom account in one click, and reloom does all the computing and writing. There are no keys to copy and nothing to configure.

### What is Human Design, in one sentence?
A map of how you are built — drawn from your exact birth moment — that shows how you take in energy, how you are designed to make decisions, and how you connect with the people around you. [Read more on reloom.life](https://reloom.life/discover).

### Which readings do I get in WordPress?
Up to thirteen solo readings per person, depending on your plan: Quick, Full, Profile, Channels, Centers, Gates, Health, Sleep, Sport, Career, Life-fit, Nervous-system regulation, and Grief & life transitions — each in a Plain voice and a Human Design voice.

### Can I do compatibility and team readings in WordPress?
Compatibility, Top matches, Teams, Job match, Plan review, Chat and Astrology live in your reloom dashboard at reloom.life — the same account the plugin is connected to. The plugin shows the solo charts and readings in wp-admin; the roster is shared, so nothing has to be re-entered.

### Is my clients' data safe?
Nothing leaves your site until you connect. After that, only the birth data needed to cast a chart is sent to reloom, over a secure connection; the plugin talks to reloom and to nobody else. See the [reloom privacy policy](https://reloom.life/privacy).

### Can my whole team use it?
Yes. On Practitioner and Studio you invite team members to one reloom account; everyone works from the same roster, in WordPress or on reloom.life.

### Does the client need a reloom account?
No. You hand them a PDF from WordPress, or a share link from reloom — they read their design without signing up for anything.
